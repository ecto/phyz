What it is

phyz is a differentiable simulation engine for articulated rigid bodies, particles, electromagnetic fields, molecular dynamics, and beyond. Every algorithm computes gradients for optimization, control, and learning.

Plug in semi-implicit Euler, RK4, or your own solver. Load MuJoCo MJCF models. Fit simulation parameters to real trajectories. Batch thousands of environments on the GPU.

The numerics come from tang: generic scalars for dual numbers and exact derivatives, dense linear algebra, and a tensor IR that lowers to CUDA, Metal, Vulkan, and WGSL. phyz is the physics on top.

phyz is early, ambitious, and open source.

A double pendulum, with gradients

Build a model, step it, and ask for the Jacobian of the next state with respect to this one. Chain those through a trajectory and you have end-to-end gradients.

Sixteen demos, running in this tab

Every card is a phyz crate compiled to WASM and drawn live. The readouts are the simulation's own numbers. Where a crate has a derivative, the card shows it doing work.

01 · Bodies

Articulated rigid bodies on Featherstone's spatial algebra. O(n) forward dynamics, analytical Jacobians, contacts through GJK/EPA.

02 · Particles

Discrete elements, the material point method, and mass-spring bodies. Grains, sand, and a jelly.

03 · Fields

Grids instead of bodies. Maxwell on a Yee lattice, Boltzmann on D2Q9, and a thousand pendulums as one field.

04 · Robots

Articulated trees under control. Torques in, the same O(n) dynamics out, one body or a hundred at once.

05 · Gradients

Every simulation on this page is differentiable. These three put the gradient to work.
